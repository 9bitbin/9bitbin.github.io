HOW TO PLAY

Tap SPACE, CLICK or TAP to fire your thrusters and climb. Gravity does the rest.

  • Thread the gaps between the neon towers — each one cleared is +1.
  • Grab glowing ★ stars for bonus points. Chain them quickly for a rising chime.
  • The ground rail and the towers are deadly. The sky above is not — but the towers are taller than the screen.
  • Towers get closer, faster and later start to drift. Chevrons on a tower cap mean it moves.
  • Survive to travel through new zones. The night never ends.
