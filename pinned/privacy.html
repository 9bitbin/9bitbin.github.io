Privacy and your data

Plain language, written for the people using Pinned. Last updated 20 October 2026.

The short version

  • Pinned is for a small group of people you invite with a code.
  • There are no ads and no third-party tracking.
  • Your live location is never saved. Voice calls are not recorded.
  • You can export everything from your board, and you can leave or delete it.

What is stored

  • Sign-in: the email address you signed in with, so you can get back in on a new device.
  • Your profile: your display name, color, optional bio and profile picture.
  • Your board: notes, replies, reactions, drawings, photos, voice notes, chat messages, background choices, playlist links, daily question answers, and check-ins.
  • Notifications: a push subscription for each device you turn notifications on for, so the app can send you alerts.

What is not stored

  • Live location: shared only while the app is open and never saved. Nobody can see past locations.
  • Voice calls: audio travels directly between phones while you're in a call. Nothing is recorded.
  • Passwords: Pinned doesn't use passwords. Sign-in is by emailed link.

Who can see what

  • Everyone on your board can see the board's notes, messages and photos.
  • Nobody outside your board can see them, and the app doesn't show your email address to other people.
  • Your hosting providers (GitHub Pages for the app, Supabase for the data and photos) keep normal server logs, as all hosting does.

Your choices

  • Export your board: Settings, then "Export this board". You get a zip with every note, reply and photo.
  • Mute: turn off notifications for any kind of activity in Settings.
  • Leave or delete a board: Settings, then "Leave this board" or "Delete board and start over".
  • Live location: turn it off at any time in the side panel.
